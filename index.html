Our Journey

✦ ✦ ✦
Lucknow♡Assam

Ek shehar Nawabon ka, ek zameen chai ke baghon ki. Beech me hazaaron kilometre, aur 8 saal ka sabr.

SAAL 1

Pehla Hello

Lucknow ki tehzeeb aur Assam ki saadgi ek chhote se hello par mili. Kisko khabar thi ke yahi hello zindagi ban jayega.

SAAL 2 - 3

Raaton ki Baatein

Assam me suraj pehle uthta hai, Lucknow me baad me. Par call par dono ki subah aur raat ek saath guzarti rahi.

SAAL 4 - 5

Sabr ka Imtihan

Door rehna aasan nahi tha. Kai baar milna adhura raha, par bharosa aur dua kabhi adhure nahi hue.

SAAL 6 - 7

Gharon tak Baat

Dil ki baat dono ghar tak pahunchi. Badon ki dua aur razamandi se rasta saaf hota gaya.

SAAL 8

Allah ka Faisla

Jo dua 8 saal pehle shuru hui thi, wo Nikah ki shakl me qubool hui. Alhamdulillah.
